--- layout: default title: nomouse permalink: /nomouse/ app: nomouse tagline: Click anything on screen without touching the mouse. A free, open-source Homerow alternative for macOS and Linux. install_note: Installs nomouse.app on macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel), or the nomouse binary on Linux (x86_64 and aarch64, X11 and Wayland), for your user, after checking its SHA-256. No sudo. summary: Press a hotkey, and every button, link, tab and field gets a short label. Type the label and nomouse clicks it. features: - title: Hints text: "ctrl+space labels everything clickable in the focused window; type a label to click it, Escape to cancel." - title: Continuous mode text: "ctrl+shift+space keeps labelling after every click, so you can work through a whole form or calculator." - title: Scroll and search text: "ctrl+alt+space picks a scrollable area for h j k l, gg and G; ctrl+alt+/ finds an element by its text." - title: Open source text: "Written in Rust, MIT or Apache-2.0, no telemetry.
