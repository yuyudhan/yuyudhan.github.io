I have been on GitHub as [yuyudhan](https://github.com/yuyudhan) since 2012. Most of what I make is small, fast and driven from the keyboard, and most of it is open source.

## What I'm working on

- **[toli](https://github.com/yuyudhan/toli)** turns the coding agent you already use into a small team: one lead you talk to, background agents that do the work, and proof before anything is called done.
- **[nomouse](/nomouse/)** lets you click anything on screen without the mouse, on macOS and Linux.
- **[quickhop](/quickhop/)** is a keyboard-first launcher for macOS and Linux that runs Raycast script commands unchanged.
- **[Simple Voice](https://github.com/yuyudhan/simple-voice)** lets you speak anywhere on your Mac and puts clean, formatted text where you are typing.

Around those sit smaller tools: [LazyTables](https://github.com/yuyudhan/LazyTables), a terminal SQL client with vim-style keys; push-to-talk dictation for [OpenCode](https://github.com/yuyudhan/opencode-speech-to-text) and [oh-my-pi](https://github.com/yuyudhan/omp-speech-to-text); and firmware configs for a Corne split keyboard in [RMK](https://github.com/yuyudhan/yuyudhan-rmk-config) and [ZMK](https://github.com/yuyudhan/yuyudhan-zmk-config). Older work includes Verhoeff checksum libraries in [Rust](https://github.com/yuyudhan/verhoeff.rs), [Go](https://github.com/yuyudhan/verhoeff.go) and [JavaScript](https://github.com/yuyudhan/verhoeff). The [projects page](/projects/) lists everything.
